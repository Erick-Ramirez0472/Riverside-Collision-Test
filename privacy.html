Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Overview

Riverside Collision Repair ("we," "us," "our") operates this website to help customers in Utah County request collision repair quotes and learn about our services. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your vehicle's year, make, and model
  • Whether you're going through insurance, and if so, your insurance company and claim number
  • Photos you upload of your vehicle's damage, VIN, and any insurance estimate or quote documents
  • Any details you type into the damage description field

We do not collect payment information, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive government ID numbers through this site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your quote request and estimate your repair
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text about your request or an active repair
  • Coordinate with your insurance company on your behalf, if you've asked us to

We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How Information Is Shared

We use a small number of service providers to run this site and process quote requests, who process data on our behalf:

  • Form and email delivery — your quote request and attached photos are sent to us via Cloudflare Workers and Resend, an email delivery service.
  • Website hosting — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Fonts — this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of that request.

If you tell us you're working with an insurance company, we may share relevant repair and claim details with that insurer to coordinate your claim. Otherwise, we don't share your information with third parties except as required by law.

Data Retention

We keep the information from your quote request for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, for the duration of our business relationship and any period required for our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see "Your Choices" below.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below. We'll respond to reasonable requests as promptly as we can.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes in the future, we'll update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through this site. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
